# Word report output specification

## Reader and purpose

Write for Pimax leadership and the country execution team. Leadership must understand the current market, product priority, competitive gap, resource allocation, expected outcome, and risk. The execution team must be able to start work without translating vague strategy.

## Recommended structure

1. Title, scope, report date, data cutoff, and core conclusions.
2. Country market baseline and implications.
3. Competitor channel and funnel teardown, starting with Meta Quest 3 and then Valve Steam Frame.
4. One integrated competitor-to-Pimax comparison matrix.
5. Pimax current operations and internal-data diagnosis.
6. Local-language keyword evidence and content implications.
7. Strategic choice: audience, use case, product priority, positioning, and key bottleneck.
8. Execution plan by audience and channel.
9. Content, advertising, KOL, community, retail and ecommerce, B2B, and exhibition playbooks.
10. 30-day, 90-day, and 6-month roadmap with owners, deliverables, KPI, decision rules, and dependencies.
11. Measurement plan, risks, facts, hypotheses, missing data, and source appendix.

Use connected prose for analysis. Use tables only for true comparisons, numeric summaries, resource allocation, and implementation registers. Keep competitor evidence, Pimax gap, and recommended action in the same row or narrative sequence.

Do not create a separate “leadership quick-read” page by default. The formal report itself must be understandable to leadership: conclusions appear early, but the evidence and implementation logic remain in the same report. Avoid a sequence of small disconnected tables that forces the reader to reconstruct the argument across pages.

All audience, platform, content, community, KOL and exhibition sections must follow [execution-cards.md](execution-cards.md). The schemas are acceptance criteria, not optional prompts.

The competitor section must include two levels: a full seven-part, channel-by-channel teardown for Meta Quest 3 and Valve Steam Frame, then a shorter local relevance teardown for one to three supplementary competitors selected by evidence. If public evidence for a channel cannot be verified, show the checked scope and the verification backlog instead of omitting the row.

The Pimax operating section must provide a consolidated map of Covered, Missing coverage, Covered but underperforming and Unknown across sales, products, ads, pages, SEO, content, social, KOL, affiliate, community, retail, ecommerce, CRM, B2B, events, service and measurement. Each diagnosis must separate fact, phenomenon, possible cause, validation hypothesis and action.

## Execution specificity

For every channel, specify:

- Audience segment and use case.
- Funnel role and objective.
- Exact campaign, series, activation, or partnership.
- Example German-language title, hook, ad angle, or event message, with an English or Chinese explanation if useful.
- Format, cadence, destination, CTA, offer, conversion event, and tracking method.
- Required asset, owner type, lead time, and dependency.
- KPI and a threshold or decision rule. If no baseline exists, define a learning KPI and how the baseline will be established.

For paid search, separate actual account keywords or search terms from proposed test hypotheses. Group hypotheses by brand, category, product, use case, competitor, problem, and B2B intent. Do not propose negative keywords without a search-term report.

For communities, name verified communities when available and include language, topic, visible size, activity, access rules, commercial norms, and the contribution approach. If a named community cannot be verified, provide selection criteria rather than inventing a target.

For KOLs, distinguish competitor-observed creators, existing Pimax partners, and proposed recruits. Include creator archetype, content concept, deliverable, disclosure, CTA, tracking, reuse rights, and success rule.

For exhibitions, cover pre-event appointment generation, on-site demo and lead capture, and post-event qualification and nurture. Include target attendee, product demo, proof asset, lead fields, owner, SLA, and pipeline KPI.

## Budget without absolute spend

When no approved budget is available, give percentage allocation and reversible test bands rather than invented currency amounts. Separate stable activity, experiments, production, local activation, and contingency. Tie reallocation to measured evidence.

## Decision register format

Each major recommendation should resolve to:

`Priority | Evidence | Decision | Deliverable | Owner | Timing | KPI | Stop or scale rule | Dependency`

## Evidence labels

Use these labels consistently:

- Fact: directly supported by the cited source or supplied dataset.
- Inference: interpretation of facts, with confidence and alternative explanation when material.
- Recommendation: proposed action, not a claim about current reality.
- Missing: decision-relevant evidence not available.

Public sources should be footnoted or endnoted with direct URLs and access dates. Internal sources should show file name, time window, and definition limits.

When the deliverable is Word, apply [word-format.md](word-format.md) as the visual and navigation acceptance standard.
