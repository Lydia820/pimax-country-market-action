# Research standard

## Evidence hierarchy

Use the strongest available source and disclose when the evidence is only a proxy.

1. Pimax internal exports supplied by the user, with definitions and coverage limits.
2. Competitor filings, investor relations, official product, pricing, store, newsroom, ad-library, social, support, and event pages.
3. Official retailer, marketplace, trade-show, association, and platform pages.
4. Established research firms and reputable trade or business publications.
5. Public creator, community, review, search, and social observations.

Record title, publisher, URL or file path, observed or publication date, geographic scope, time window, and the exact claim supported.

## Competitor teardown

Start with Meta Quest 3 and Valve Steam Frame. Then screen local candidates and add one to three supplementary competitors only when at least one of these is evidenced: material local retail visibility, direct premium PCVR overlap, professional simulation relevance, or control of an important marketplace, specialist or B2B route. Record why each selected competitor matters and why excluded candidates were not prioritized.

For Meta Quest 3 and Valve Steam Frame, answer each point with country-specific evidence where possible:

- Audience and jobs to be done: mainstream standalone VR, PCVR, simulation, entertainment, fitness, family, enterprise, education, training, design, or location-based use.
- Product and offer: hardware, bundles, subscriptions, financing, promotions, warranty, returns, shipping, localized language, and payment methods.
- Distribution: direct store, marketplaces, electronics retail, specialist shops, distributors, B2B partners, demos, and stock visibility.
- Paid media: Google Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube, Meta, TikTok, Twitch, programmatic, retail media, OOH, and creator whitelisting. Capture active examples, first-seen or longevity when visible, format, message, CTA, and destination. Do not infer spend or performance.
- Owned and earned content: website, SEO, news, email, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, Reddit, Steam, media reviews, PR, and comparison pages. Capture exact examples and observable engagement when visible.
- KOL and affiliate: creator type, audience, content format, product, message, disclosure, CTA or code, posting cadence, and observable response. Identify reusable patterns, not content to copy.
- Community: official and third-party Discord, Reddit, Facebook groups, forums, Steam community, simulation or game communities, topic, language, visible membership, activity, and commercial norms.
- Offline: exhibitions, retail demos, roadshows, esports or simulation activations, enterprise conferences, and local partnerships.
- Funnel: ad or content → landing or store page → proof → offer → checkout or lead form → remarketing → support or onboarding.
- Financial context: relevant company or segment revenue, losses, investment priorities, installed-base or ecosystem disclosures. State scope and never derive country sales without evidence.

For every observed channel or operating action, record in one comparable chain:

1. What the competitor did.
2. Why it may work for the local audience or funnel stage.
3. The dated public evidence and any observable result.
4. Whether Pimax already covers the same function.
5. The specific gap.
6. What is transferable and what is structurally unavailable or unsuitable.
7. The exact Pimax action, owner, KPI and validation method.

Check, rather than assume, Google Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Reddit, Discord, Twitch, vertical media, affiliates, retail media, Amazon or local marketplaces, DOOH and traditional media. When an ad library, account or public archive does not expose a reliable country snapshot, write "not publicly verified" and propose the verification method; never turn silence into an absence claim.

Capture content platform, theme, format, cadence when observable, product proposition, CTA, destination and high-performing examples with public metrics. Capture landing, comparison, purchase and reseller pages; demos; delivery; returns; warranty and repair. Capture views, engagement, search visibility, rank, ratings, review count, stock, promotion frequency, community scale, retail coverage and public case data only as signals, never verified sales unless the platform says so.

## Pimax operating analysis

Analyze supplied country data before proposing solutions:

- Sales: period, gross and net definitions, orders versus units, product mix, monthly trend, cancellation or return adjustments, AOV if valid, store and channel coverage, and anomalies.
- Paid media: platform coverage, currency, spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, attributed conversions or value, conversion-action definitions, campaign trend, product mapping, and data gaps.
- KOL: signed versus activated, stage distribution, content delivered, audience and country fit, attribution coverage, sales or lead contribution where available, and owner completeness.
- Other operations: local website and SEO, marketplace and retail presence, social and content cadence, community coverage, CRM and email, affiliate or channel partners, B2B lead generation, events, local service, and measurement.

Classify each area:

- Covered and performing: verified activity with evidence of useful outcomes.
- Covered but needs optimization: activity exists but has a measurable weakness or unresolved efficiency question.
- Missing coverage: competitor or market evidence indicates a meaningful channel or funnel step Pimax does not visibly cover.
- Unknown: evidence is insufficient to judge. Unknown is not a failure score.

For each area, explain in order: fact, observed phenomenon, possible cause, hypothesis still requiring validation and recommended action. Organize the final diagnosis under Covered, Missing coverage, Covered but underperforming and Unknown so no issue remains hidden in narrative.

## Local keyword evidence

Run at least two Google Trends comparison sets for the target country and a stated period:

- Brand or product set: the two fixed competitors, Pimax, and the most relevant generic local-language term.
- Use-case set: local-language terms for VR headset, PCVR, virtual reality, flight simulation, sim racing, and any country-specific high-intent use case.

Capture average relative interest, notable peaks, rising or related queries, and subregional patterns only when Google exposes enough data. Google Trends values are normalized indices within the chosen comparison set, not absolute search volume; small changes in the comparison set can change the values. State when a term has insufficient data.

Supplement Trends with:

- Google or retailer search suggestions and category labels.
- Public retailer titles, reviews and comparison language.
- Public Reddit, Discord, forum, YouTube and media discussion phrases.
- Pimax account search-term data when available.

Classify every keyword as one of: observed trend term, observed discussion term, proposed SEO/content term, proposed paid-search test, or actual account search term. Map each term or cluster to audience, funnel stage, content asset, landing page, CTA and measurement. Proposed terms are hypotheses until search-term and conversion data validate them.

## Strategy synthesis

Translate evidence into choices:

- Which audience and use case offer the clearest right to win?
- Which Pimax product should lead, which supports it, and which should not receive broad acquisition spend yet?
- Where should Pimax compete directly, and where should it avoid Quest's ecosystem or price frame?
- Which competitor practice is transferable, which requires adaptation, and which is structurally unavailable to Pimax?
- Which bottleneck is reach, consideration, conversion, distribution, trust, service, measurement, or retention?
- What must be true for the plan to work, and what evidence would disprove the strategy?

## Public sales and ecommerce evidence

Check direct stores, Amazon, major national electronics retailers, specialist VR or PC retailers, price-comparison sites, Steam or platform stores, and B2B distributors. Capture price, stock, seller, delivery, rating, review count, rank if visible, promotion, bundle, warranty, localization, and purchase friction.

These are evidence supplements inside distribution, demand, competitor, and funnel analysis. Do not create a detached ecommerce chapter unless the user requests it.
