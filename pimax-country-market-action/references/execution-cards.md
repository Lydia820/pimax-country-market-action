# Mandatory execution cards

Use these schemas in every country report. Combine adjacent fields in a readable table or connected prose when necessary, but do not omit them. Mark unavailable facts as Missing and proposed values as Test hypothesis.

## Audience card

For each material B2C or B2B audience record:

- Audience and purchase or usage scenario.
- Main need and pain point.
- Recommended Pimax product and why.
- Platforms where the audience can be reached.
- Most effective content theme and required proof.
- Main buying resistance.
- CTA.
- Destination or landing page.

Cover, when locally relevant: hard-core PCVR, flight simulation, racing simulation, Quest upgraders, resolution-first users, Micro OLED or lightweight users, premium cockpit owners, creators or technical reviewers, arcades or experience venues, and education, training, industrial or professional institutions.

## Platform execution card

Create one card for each material platform, including an explicit Hold or Not priority decision when a platform should not be used:

- Platform.
- Audience.
- Lead product.
- Marketing objective and funnel role.
- Content or ad format.
- Exact topic, hook, keyword cluster or advertising proposition.
- Publishing or media cadence.
- Destination.
- CTA.
- Tracking fields and conversion event.
- Core KPI.
- Test period or sample-read date.
- Stop, fix, optimize or scale rule.

Check Google Search, Google Shopping and Performance Max, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Reddit, Discord, Twitch, vertical forums, local gaming and technology media, KOL and affiliate, Amazon and local ecommerce, retailers and demo locations, email or CRM, and SEO or localized owned content. Do not force an active recommendation when evidence supports Hold.

## Content pillar card

Define three to five pillars. For each include:

- Pillar, lead product and audience.
- Search or discussion evidence.
- Directly producible titles.
- Video or article structure.
- Test evidence to show.
- CTA and destination.
- Local-language and cultural notes.
- What can be reused internationally and what must be recreated locally.
- Cadence, owner and KPI.

The complete set must address product selection, same-PC task comparisons, flight and racing tasks, two-hour comfort, RTX configuration and performance, setup and troubleshooting, local pricing, shipping, returns, warranty and repair, and retail or demo purchase paths.

## Community card

For each named community include:

- Name, platform, language, topic and visible member or activity signal with date.
- Access and commercial-content rules, or state that moderator confirmation is required.
- Recommended contribution and joint activity.
- Lead capture method.
- Order tracking method.
- First deliverable, owner and success rule.

## KOL card

Separate observed competitor creators, existing Pimax partners and proposed recruits. For every archetype include:

- Audience fit and content direction.
- Recommended product.
- Deliverables and publishing requirements.
- Disclosure, review-independence and claim guardrails.
- CTA, unique link or code and destination.
- Click, order, cancellation and refund feedback.
- Usage or paid amplification rights.
- Repeat, rework or stop rule.

## Exhibition and offline card

For each event include:

- Name, date, city and theme.
- Attendee type and product fit.
- Verified competitor participation, not observed after checking, or unknown.
- Recommended role: exhibit, sponsor, speak, attend, host a side event or partner display.
- Product and demo scenario.
- Pre-event target list, invitation and appointment plan.
- On-site experience flow and staffing.
- Lead fields and consent.
- Follow-up SLA and nurture path.
- Budget level or cost components when no quote is public.
- KPI and continue or stop rule.

Badge scans and foot traffic are not success metrics. Use qualified conversations, appointments, opportunities, cost per qualified meeting and attributed or influenced pipeline.
